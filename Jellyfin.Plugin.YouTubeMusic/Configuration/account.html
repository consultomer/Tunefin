Connect your YouTube Music account

Once connected, with your YouTube account:

  • Searching in Jellyfin / Finamp also shows YouTube Music songs, and you can play them.
  • Songs you ♥ are downloaded into your own library, Liked – your name. Other users can't see it.
  • Your YouTube Music “Liked songs” are imported into that library.

Until you connect, Jellyfin works as usual: search shows only songs already on the server. A song several people like is stored only once on the server.

  1. On a computer, open a private / incognito window and sign in at music.youtube.com with your account.
  2. Export the cookies for youtube.com in Netscape / cookies.txt format, e.g. with the “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY” browser extension (allow it in private windows).
  3. Paste the file's contents below (or pick the file), then click Save & import.
  4. Close the private window without signing out, so the exported session stays valid.

The cookies are stored on this server only for reading your liked list. Anyone with them can act as your YouTube account, so only use this on a server you trust. Click “Disconnect” to delete them.
