Tunefin architecture

How the Jellyfin plugin, the sidecar and the two shared folders fit together when Jellyfin runs directly on the server, without containers.

What happens on each action

Search

  1. The client searches Jellyfin as usual.
  2. The search filter asks the sidecar's /search.
  3. Matches you already have are dropped; the rest are added after your local results.

Play

  1. The song is an item of the YouTube Music channel.
  2. Jellyfin plays /stream/:id from the sidecar.
  3. The sidecar resolves the URL with yt-dlp and proxies googlevideo with Range support.

Like (♥)

  1. The likes listener sends the song to /download.
  2. The sidecar stores one copy in .store/ and hardlinks it into the user's folder.
  3. Jellyfin scans it into that user's Liked library.

Import likes

  1. At startup and every 6 hours, the import task calls /likes.
  2. The sidecar reads the user's "Liked music" playlist with youtubei.js.
  3. Songs the user doesn't have yet are downloaded like a ♥.
